Overview

Payment processing is powered by Stripe, we offer a seamless experience to all customers on your store using this gateway method. All transactions are performed securely.

Once a payment gateway has been setup customers will be able to begin using your store to place orders.

To process payments on your store every transaction is charged at 6% of the order value plus a 30p base charge. For example an order of £20 would incur a fee of £1.50 for processing.

Transaction fees can be reduced by setting up a monthly subscription after you have added your gateway. With an active and paid subscription the transaction fee is reduced to 3.5%, this would mean a £20 order would incur a fee of £1 saving 50p compared with a non-subscription based transaction.
