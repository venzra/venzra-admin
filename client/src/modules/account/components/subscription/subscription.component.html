Overview

Paying or a monthly subscription can reduce your monthly running cost significantly, once you are happy with your account we recommend setting up a subscription, however it is not a requirement to operate your store and generate sales.

Our subscriptions are based on a pre-paid credits system. Every credit is translated to a charge based on your selected plan. For example a plan that costs £1.50 per credit would mean that 10 credits equals £15. We will always inform you how much your monthly costs are in your chosen currency however when selecting your subscription options we will reference a cost in credits.

Unused credits cannot be converted back to currency, cancellation of a subscription in any given period cannot be refunded.

The minimum monthly charge for an active subscription is 20 credits.

Warning, should your account fall into arrears on your subscription you will revert automatically to paying transaction fees based on a non-subscription account.
