Overview

Subscriptions are paid for on a monthly basis, every month your account will be debited and you will received credits to use on the features for the platform. The fee charged is based on your selected currency, however all credits within the platform are worth the same value.

To make things simpler for our past and future user plans, we base our subscriptions on a credits system. Every credit is then translated to a charge based on your selected plan. For example a plan that costs $1.50 per credit would mean that 10 credits equals $15. We will always inform you how much your monthly costs are in your chosen currency however when selecting your subscription options we will reference a cost in credits.

Unused credits cannot be converted back to currency, cancellation of a subscription in any given period cannot be refunded.

As a trial account, the first month of your subscription is free of charge, this means on signing up you will not start to pay until the same day on the next calendar month, for example signing up on 14th April would mean your first payment is due on 14th May and would be for the period 14th May at 00:00 till 13th June at 23:59 inclusive.

The minimum monthly charge for an active subscription is 20 credits.
