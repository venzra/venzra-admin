Customise contact form

Configure where contact emails are sent, and the options available. Be careful not to enable too many fields on your contact form as this can become a barrier to customers sending you a message. Any additional fields will be offered to customers as optional fields unless otherwise stated.
Configuration options Additional information capture options for your site when customers are using the contact form. This should be an email address on a domain owned by you where messages sent by your customers will be received and processed. A valid email address is required.

Full name

The full name to be provided by the customer. Required field.

Company name

Ask for the customer to provide a company name if they are a business.

Email address

The email address provided by the customer to respond to them with. Required field.

Phone number

You may prefer to contact customers by phone when they contact you, this optionally ask customers to provide a phone number.

Subject

Allow customer to provide a subject line for their message.

Reason for contact

Ask why the customer is contacting you, we will provide customers a list of common reasons to contact you.

Message content

A free text field from which the customer can provide details. Required field.
