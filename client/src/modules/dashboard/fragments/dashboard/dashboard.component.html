Products are a core feature for any online store, they are used to allow customers to purchase physical or digital goods. Products may even be services such as a pre-paid appointment or experience.

Add as many products are you are wishing to sell, the more products available to customers will improve search engine results and increase the interest and reach of your store to make sales.

The final delivery method is up to you to decide, this is a mechinism to configure what choices you would like to make available to customers for the typical delivery costs you would expect to incur.

Note that shipping options may also come with cart value requirements; you do not need to use this, however cart values can trigger the availability of a shipping option. Be careful to ensure there will always be at least one option available to a customer.

Having a payment gateway is required to allow customers to purchase goods at the checkout. Without a gateway setup and active, customers are not able to place orders.

The payment gateway also is how you will be paid for orders placed, once a payment has been authorised the gateway will transfer money to the account you provid them with, on a schedule you have given them.
